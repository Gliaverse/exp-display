Maple EXP Tracker

A minimal, personal-use EXP tracker for MapleStory Classic World. Capture and OCR run entirely in your browser—no MapleStory process or memory access, and no session data is saved to disk.

Browser notice: Chrome or Edge is recommended. Firefox produced stale screen-capture frames during project testing.
